Pharrell Williams co-founded the production duo the Neptunes with Chad Hugo. Their work spans hip-hop, pop, and R&B.

His song “Happy” appeared in Despicable Me 2 and became a worldwide hit.

Williams is also part of N.E.R.D and has worked in fashion, including as a creative director for Louis Vuitton.

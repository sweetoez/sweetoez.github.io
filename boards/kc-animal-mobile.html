Koi are domesticated ornamental carp, selectively bred for vivid colors and patterns. They are commonly kept in outdoor garden ponds.

Well-cared-for koi can live for decades. Reports of individual fish living beyond 200 years are exceptional and difficult to verify.

Hanako, a scarlet koi from Japan, is often reported to have lived 226 years. The extraordinary age is famous, but not independently verified.

Kohaku koi are white with red markings; bright colors can also make them visible to predators.

Janelle Monáe

American singer and actor

Janelle Monáe is an American singer, songwriter, and actor. Her work includes the albums The ArchAndroid and Dirty Computer, and films Moonlight and Hidden Figures.

Monáe's 2018 album Dirty Computer was accompanied by a 48-minute science-fiction film she called an “emotion picture.” The film explores identity and self-expression through a futuristic story.

Her screen roles include Moonlight and Hidden Figures, followed by leading roles in Harriet and Antebellum, and a supporting part in Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery.

Monáe played Mary Jackson in Hidden Figures and Teresa in Moonlight, both released in 2016. She later appeared in Harriet, Antebellum, and Glass Onion.
